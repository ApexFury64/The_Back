1. Cell Structure & Function

Diving deep into plant vs animal cells and understanding cell organelles.

What is a Cell?

The cell is the basic structural, functional, and biological unit of all known organisms. Cells are often called the "building blocks of life". All living things are composed of one or more cells, and cells arise from pre-existing cells through division.

🔬

Cell Types

Prokaryotes: Simple cells without a nucleus (e.g. Bacteria).
Eukaryotes: Complex cells containing a distinct nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.

Core Cell Organelles

Organelle Nickname Function
Nucleus The Control Center Contains the cell's DNA and coordinates all cellular activities.
Mitochondria The Powerhouse Converts nutrients into energy (ATP) through cellular respiration.
Chloroplast The Solar Panel (Plants only) Captures sunlight to generate glucose through photosynthesis.
