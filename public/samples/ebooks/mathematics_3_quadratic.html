3. Quadratic Equations

Mastering parabolas, polynomial factoring, and the quadratic formula.

What is a Quadratic Equation?

A quadratic equation is a second-order polynomial equation in a single variable. When plotted on a graph, it creates a curve called a parabola (a U-shaped curve that opens upward or downward). The standard form of a quadratic equation is:

ax² + bx + c = 0

The Quadratic Formula

To find the roots (x-intercepts where y = 0), we can utilize the famous Quadratic Formula:

x = -b ± √(b² - 4ac)
