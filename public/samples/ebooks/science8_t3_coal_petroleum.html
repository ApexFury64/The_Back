Coal and Petroleum Resources

Understanding fossil fuels formation, coal distillation, petroleum refining, and conservation guides.

Natural Resources & Fossil Fuels

Natural resources are categorized into two types: Inexhaustible (sunlight, air) and Exhaustible (forests, minerals, coal, petroleum). Coal, petroleum, and natural gas are formed from the dead remains of living organisms buried millions of years ago, which is why they are called fossil fuels.

🌳

Carbonisation

The slow chemical process of conversion of dead vegetation into coal under high pressure and temperature inside the earth's crust is called carbonisation.

Products of Coal Distillation

When coal is heated in the absence of air (destructive distillation), it yields useful products:

  • Coke: A tough, porous, and black substance. It is an almost pure form of carbon used in steel manufacturing and metal extraction.
  • Coal Tar: A thick black liquid with an unpleasant smell. It is a mixture of about 200 substances, used to synthesize dyes, drugs, explosives, perfumes, and roofing materials.
  • Coal Gas: Obtained during the processing of coal. Used as an industrial fuel and formerly for street lighting.

Petroleum Refining

Petroleum is a dark, oily liquid. It is a complex mixture of petroleum gas, petrol, diesel, kerosene, paraffin wax, and lubricating oil. The process of separating these various fractions by their boiling points is called refining, which is done in fractional distillation columns.

Fraction Common Use
LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) Fuel for home and industry cooking.
Petrol Motor fuel, aviation fuel, solvent for dry cleaning.
Kerosene Fuel for stoves, lamps, and jet aircraft.
Diesel Fuel for heavy motor vehicles, generators, and tractors.
