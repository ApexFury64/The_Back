1. Introduction to Algebra

Demystifying variables, balancing equations, and solving for the unknown.

What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics dealing with symbols and the rules for manipulating those symbols. Instead of using concrete numbers, we use letters called variables to represent unknown values. This allows us to write general formulas and solve complex real-world equations.

x

The Variable

A letter like x, y, or z that acts as a placeholder for a value we want to find.

💡 The Golden Rule of Algebra

Whatever you do to one side of the equation, you must do to the other side. To keep the equation balanced, if you add a number to the left side, you must add the exact same number to the right side!
